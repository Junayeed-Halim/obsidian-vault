**Agile Process for Software Development **

The agile process is based on the well-known [four agile values](https://www.productboard.com/glossary/agile-values) (Agile Manifesto 2001). The light-weight agile process for the Software Development Studio is organised as a 12-week teaching period. See subject outline for details. Here, is a typical agile process, which is organised into 4 sprints plus sprint 0 (note the sprint-to-week mapping).

Week 2 studio activity:

- This week, your tutor will run some activities to get to know you, help you get to know your peers, and finalize group formation during your studio session. 
- Select from the projects available in your studio session. We will consider your preferences, technical background, skills, and experience levels across team members.
- Your tutor will share your client's contact details after team allocation.
- Team leads should reach out to the client and introduce their team (members and the roles they take) and schedule the next meeting (to discuss the project scope and deliverables) or a series of meetings before they leave the studio session.
