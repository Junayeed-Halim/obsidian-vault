## Academic integrity

To be successful at university, you must be able to work with a variety of sources and incorporate them into your work, without compromising your academic integrity. However, referencing and citing can be complicated and confusing.

Use the following tutorial to help increase your awareness of what plagiarism is and how to avoid it. This tutorial will moreover give you the tools to make sure your work meets the correct standards of citing and referencing.

If you need support with your studies or decisions, visit [plagiarism and academic integrity explained ](https://www.uts.edu.au/current-students/support/academic-support/academic-integrity)or [open 'Avoiding Plagiarism' HELPS activity in a new tab](https://avoidingplagiarism.uts.edu.au/).

For guidance on researching and referencing appropriate scholarly sources, please refer to the [Library referencing guides](https://www.lib.uts.edu.au/referencing).
