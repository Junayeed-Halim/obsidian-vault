Dear Students,

 

Welcome to Software Development Studio (SDS)! 

In this subject, you will work on real-life projects led by external clients, such as full-stack websites and mobile applications, which often incorporate AI features, signal analysis, 3D simulations, and other algorithm development projects. You will mainly be working with your peers in teams and supported by experienced teaching staff.

For those who are skilled in software development, this will be an opportunity to test and expand your capabilities on real-life projects and challenge yourself to build quality software. For those who are new to software development, this subject may be slightly challenging with a steep learning curveas you develop new skills and apply them to your project.

With real-life clients, there is always an aspect of client expectations. I want to assure you that within the subject’s framework, our priority is for you to learn and create as much as you can—you are “safe,” and you have no liability for delivery. Therefore, I encourage you to try your best and enjoy the hands-on learning process.

Many of our past projects have led to future opportunities with clients or been showcased at the UTS Tech Festival annual event, and I am happy to facilitate such engagements where suitable.
