This page gives a brief summary of the assessments you will be required to complete as part of this subjects. For a full description of assessment tasks and assessment criteria please refer to the subject outline.

## Assessment overview

**Assessment task 1: Assignment 1 - Individual | weighting 20% | due week 6
**This assessment is to allow students to demonstrate their knowledge of the concepts learnt in the subject, research skills and self-assess their progress in the subject.

The assignment is an individual written assignment based lecture contents, e.g., virtualisation technologies and the use of cloud within businesses.

**Assessment task 2: Assignment 2 - Group Major Project | weighting 40% | due week 11
**Whilst designing a cloud computing infrastructure and implementing a prototype for a small size organisation, students are expected to analyse stakeholder needs, implement and test solutions, and demonstrate their subject knowledge and communication/presentation skills.

This assignment is based on group work. Students are required to specify, design, architect, develop and implement a major infrastructure based project.

This project is divided into the following deliverables:
(1) A design and architecture report
(2) Implement/Build this infrastructure stack, noting issues of this deployment
(3) A presentation/demo on the implementation will be during the lab in week 11. This includes peer marking and critical review.

**Assessment task 3: Quiz 1 - Individual | weighting 20% | due week 8**

This assessment is to allow students to demonstrate their knowledge of cloud computing infrastructure, e.g., cloud solutions, cloud core architectures, cloud platforms, cloud computing technologies, technical limitations, and so on.

This open-book quiz consists of multiple-choice, single-answer questions and short-answer questions. Students complete the quiz during the lecture class in the week indicated.

## Academic integrity

Academic integrity fosters independent learning skills, and good practice demands personal integrity and respect for scholarship. Students should be aware of the ethical and legal requirements with regard to assessments, authorship and the acknowledgement of work done by others.  

If you need support with your studies or decisions, visit [plagiarism and academic integrity explained.](https://www.lib.uts.edu.au/node/897881)

For guidance on researching and referencing appropriate scholarly sources, please refer to the [Library referencing guides](https://www.lib.uts.edu.au/help/referencing).
