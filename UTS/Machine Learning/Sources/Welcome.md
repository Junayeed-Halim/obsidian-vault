## Welcome to Machine Learning

Machine learning is an exciting field studying how to let computers learn from experience and program themselves to better adapt to the environment and tasks. This is mainly achieve by cleverly designed algorithms to analyse data to extract information and knowledge. This course has been designed to provide you with a mixture of applied and theoretical introduction to the field of machine learning, and an orientation to its different usages.

## Delivery

The subject is delivered in weekly lecturers (10AM-12PM on Mondays). Please visit our Canvas site for information to get prepared before joining the sessions. The preparation materials will be updated during the semester. 

We will adopt an AI-open style to facilitate the learning. So mostly the preparation consists of directions and instructions to help you explore a topic using AI tools. I would attempt to tailor the investigation of the topics according to students skill level and learning experience, so the guidance will be released in a weekly manner. Please visit Canvas site often to get updated.

## Assessment Tasks

Assessment Task 1 consists of in-class quizzes.

Assessment Task 2 is to explore a machine learning-related topic  and keep a journal. The task can be either project implementation or theory study. The task is supposed to be completed by using AI assistance. I.e. AI-assistance is STRONGLY RECOMMENDED.

Assessment Task 3 is to present your findings in A2 to classmates and peer grade.

Please refer to assignments section for more details.
