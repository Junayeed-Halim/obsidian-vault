This is a demo of using resampling to estimate some statistics of a population and investigate how much the estimation can vary.

That example does not include the learning process. This does not change the nature of the problem. Keep in mind that the outputs generated by learned predictors for a certain input are stochastically determined by the training *examples*. When the (randomly drawn) training dataset changes, the prediction result changes accordingly. Therefore the prediction can be considered as a sophisticated statistics of the training data distribution. Follow the video tutorial to use resampling to understand the variance by yourself.
