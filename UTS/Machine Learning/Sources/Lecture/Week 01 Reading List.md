# Week 01 Reading List

> **Course:** [[UTS/Machine Learning|Machine Learning]]
> **Week:** 01 — Introduction to Machine Learning

---

## Required Reading

### Chapter 1: Introduction to Machine Learning
- **Book:** "An Introduction to Statistical Learning" (James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani)
- **Chapter:** 1 — Introduction
- **Focus:** What is statistical learning? The distinction between parametric and non-parametric methods. The bias-variance tradeoff introduced conceptually.

### Chapter 1: Introduction
- **Book:** "Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning" (Christopher Bishop)
- **Chapter:** 1 — Introduction
- **Focus:** Examples of pattern recognition problems. The role of probability theory in ML.

---

## Supplementary Reading

### The Discipline of Machine Learning
- **Author:** Tom Mitchell
- **Type:** Tutorial / Overview
- **Focus:** Formal definition of ML (E, T, P framework). The role of representation, evaluation, and optimisation.

### A Few Useful Things to Know About Machine Learning
- **Author:** Pedro Domingos (2012)
- **Type:** Article
- **Focus:** Practical insights: data is more important than algorithms, overfitting is the central challenge, feature engineering matters, etc.

### The Bias-Variance Decomposition
- **Source:** Various (see ISL Chapter 2, ESL Chapter 7)
- **Focus:** Mathematical decomposition of expected prediction error. Understanding high bias vs. high variance.

---

## Online Resources

- **Coursera: Machine Learning** (Andrew Ng) — Week 1 lectures on introduction and linear regression.
- **fast.ai: Practical Deep Learning** — top-down approach; good for intuition but different from this course's bottom-up style.
- **scikit-learn documentation** — "Supervised learning" section for overview of algorithms.

---

## Note

These readings are supplementary. The primary course material is the lecture notes and tutorial exercises. Focus on understanding the concepts rather than reading every word of every reference.
