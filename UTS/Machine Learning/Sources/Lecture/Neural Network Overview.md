## Neural Networks

In this module, you are introduced to the idea of combining multiple perceptrons into a new model family. By "combining", we mean 3-fold expansion:

1. Assembling multiple perceptrons into a group to process the information (and therefore has more than one output)

2. Stack the groups one on top of another to form multi-stage processing pipelines

3. Investigate different activation and structure designs.

This gives the powerful family of neural networks. We will study the construction, computation and training of neural networks. You will have both hands-on experiences of building neural network models and knowledge of the state-of-the-art NN models in different application areas.

We provide a series of hands-on implementation of neural networks in this module.

For the theories and computational models, it is strongly recommended to check out online materials on 3Blue1Brown ([link](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks)) before coming to the session.

 

### **Topics you'll cover:**

- Neural network structure
- Computations in neural networks and how they are formulated in matrix operations
- Back-propagation algorithm
- Neural network training
