The design of perceptrons draw its inspiration from the biological neural systems, where a neuron receives stimulation signals from its synaptic connections to other neurons and determines its own state. When the accumulated stimuli exceed some threshold, the neuron is excited and fires an output signal.

As a machine learning technique, the term perception refers to both a family of data models as well as the learning algorithm associated with the family. We will consider the data models in this section before we turn to the learning algorithm in the next.
